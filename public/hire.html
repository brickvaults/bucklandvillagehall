The spaces

Room for every occasion

Book the main hall, the small hall or the whole building, and make the most of the garden in the warmer months.

Main hall

A light, high-ceilinged hall with a stage, wooden floor and arched windows. Ideal for receptions, parties, performances and classes.

Small hall

A cosy second room with its own kitchenette. Great for meetings, small groups, or as a bar and lounge alongside the main hall.

Garden

A large lawned garden with a BBQ and seating area. Perfect for summer parties, children's games and drinks in the sunshine.

What's included

Everything you need, on site

  • Fully modernised kitchen
  • Stage
  • Toilets, including accessible
  • Tables and chairs
  • Crockery, cutlery & glasses
  • Free WiFi by Gigaclear
  • Garden with BBQ & seating
  • Two car parks at the front
