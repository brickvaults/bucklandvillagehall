About the hall

A classic village hall in a picturesque setting

Buckland Memorial Hall is a classic Cotswold stone village hall. It's used by clubs, classes and group meetings, and can be booked for weddings and other private events.

  • Main hall with stage
  • Small hall with kitchenette
  • Fully modernised kitchen
  • Toilets, including accessible
  • Lawned garden with BBQ & seating
  • Two car parks at the front
  • Tables, chairs & crockery to hire
  • Free WiFi throughout
With thanks

Free WiFi, courtesy of Gigaclear

The WiFi in the hall is provided free of charge by Gigaclear, a local company with its HQ just down the road in Abingdon, so everyone can stay connected whatever their event. Please show your thanks by dropping by the Gigaclear website.
